# Magnum members — study list

**Status:** sourcing guide for the eye-gym deck (research doc §2 Proposal 2).
**Names only** — members' work is in-copyright, so study copies live in the
gitignored `eye-gym/corpus/` and never enter this repo. Scheduling is real
Anki (decided 2026-10-12); this list feeds card fronts via `cards.csv`.

Non-exhaustive; <https://www.magnumphotos.com/photographers/> is the roster
source of truth. Scans from books you own beat web rips for study quality.

## Street canon

- Henri Cartier-Bresson (founder) — geometry, the decisive moment
- Elliott Erwitt — visual wit, one-liners
- Josef Koudelka — grain, formal chaos, exile
- Richard Kalvar — street surrealism, off-moment
- Leonard Freed — humanist long-form (_Police Work_)
- Hiroji Kubota — Asia, decades-long patience
- Ferdinando Scianna — Sicily, fashion-as-document
- Nikos Economopoulos — Balkans, loose-limbed everyday

## Color formalists

Maps to `cards.csv` "Polyphonic Choreography" and "Webb slant rhymes".

- Alex Webb — layered color complexity, slant rhymes
- Harry Gruyaert — color as the subject itself
- Gueorgui Pinkhassov — light-first abstraction
- Constantine Manos — American color, _American Color_
- Martin Parr — flash, satire, consumerism
- Trent Parke — lyrical/rhapsodic bucket (`cards.csv`), Australian light

## Flash / confrontational

- Bruce Gilden — in-your-face flash street (closest Magnum kin to the repo's
  hardboiled flash-noir direction, `docs/design-research.md`)

## Documentary essay / humanist

- Robert Capa, David "Chim" Seymour, George Rodger (founders)
- Werner Bischof, Marc Riboud, René Burri, Eve Arnold
- Susan Meiselas — Nicaragua, Kurdish archive
- Gilles Peress — conflict as form (_Farewell to Bosnia_)
- Raymond Depardon — France, filmic patience
- Mary Ellen Mark — portraiture with subjects, not of them
- Larry Towell — Mennonites, Palestine, long immersion

## Contemporary

- Alex Majoli — theatrical daylight-flash tableaux
- Thomas Dworzak — Caucasus, archival collage
- Alec Soth — lyrical American surface (maps to "Lyrical / Rhapsodic Odyssey")
- Matt Black — American poverty, geography of inequality
- Carolyn Drake, Bieke Depoorter, Cristina de Middel
